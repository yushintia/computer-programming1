## Chain (Limits → Pain), see SPINE.md for full text

1 → 2 → 3 → 4 → 5 → 6 → 7 → (8 review) → 9 → 10 → 11 → 12 → 13 → 14 →
(15 review). No single running case study (see SPINE.md) — each week's
pain/worked example is that week's own lab exercise, deliberately
example-diverse (name card, temperature converter, grade classifier,
guessing game, student records, and so on).

## Grading

Midterm 20% (Week 8, live in-lab coding) · Project 30% (artifact due
Week 14, defended Week 15) · Final 30% (Week 15, demo + oral defense) ·
Attendance 10% (ongoing) · Assignments 10% (weekly, Weeks 2-13).
